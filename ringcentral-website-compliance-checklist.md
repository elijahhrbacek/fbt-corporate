# RingCentral SMS/10DLC — Website Compliance Checklist

A guideline for building or updating a corporate website so it meets RingCentral / carrier
requirements for SMS (text messaging) registration.

> Source: RingCentral "Registering longcode numbers for SMS/MMS messaging" guide.
> This is a simplified, non-technical version for briefing a web team.

---

## 1. General Website Requirements

- [x] Website is **live and fully accessible** (no "under construction" pages, no broken links)
      — Site structure is complete. Confirm public URL after deploy. Contact/ticket forms post to Formspree `https://formspree.io/f/xaewbveq`.
- [x] The **company/business name** shown on the website **matches exactly** the legal business
      name used in the SMS/TCR registration
      — Confirmed: **Fort Bend Tickets LLC**.
- [x] **Support contact information is visible** somewhere on the site — a phone number, email
      address, or a working contact form
- [x] **Age gate added** — only required if the business involves age-restricted products or
      content (e.g., alcohol, cannabis, adult content). Visitors must confirm their age before
      accessing the site.
      — Not required for this ticket-broker site.

---

## 2. Any Form That Collects a Phone Number

This applies to **every** web form with a phone number field — even if that field isn't
specifically meant for texting.

- [x] Form includes an **SMS opt-in checkbox**
- [x] The checkbox is **NOT pre-checked** — the visitor must actively check it themselves
- [x] SMS consent is **not bundled** with other agreements (e.g., not combined with general
      Terms acceptance or email opt-in)
- [x] The opt-in text clearly states:
  - [x] What the visitor is opting into (what type of messages)
  - [x] How **often** they'll receive messages
  - [x] That **message and data rates may apply**
  - [x] How to get **help** (e.g., "Reply HELP for help")
  - [x] How to **opt out** (e.g., "Reply STOP to opt out")
- [x] The form **links to** the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

**Example opt-in language:**
> By providing my phone number, I agree to receive SMS notifications regarding my account
> from [Business Name]. Reply STOP to opt-out; Reply HELP for help; Message and data rates
> apply; Messaging frequency may vary. See Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for additional
> details.

---

## 3. Privacy Policy

- [x] States **what personal information** is collected
- [x] States **how** that information is used
- [x] States **who** the information is shared with
- [x] Clearly states that **SMS consent/phone numbers are NOT shared with third parties** for
      their own purposes
- [x] States **message frequency**, possible carrier fees, and opt-out instructions
- [x] Is **linked in the consent language** on every form that collects a phone number
- [x] Is **easily accessible site-wide** — best practice is a link in the footer of every page

---

## 4. Terms of Service

- [x] Includes **SMS-specific disclosures** (not just general terms)
- [x] Explains **how a visitor opts in** to SMS
- [x] Explains **what type of messages** will be sent
- [x] States **message frequency**
- [x] States **message/data rate** notice
- [x] Includes **link to the Privacy Policy**
- [x] Includes **Help instructions**
- [x] Includes **Opt-out instructions**
- [x] If Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are separate documents, the SMS section in
      Terms of Service **links to** the Privacy Policy
- [x] Is **easily accessible site-wide** — best practice is a link in the footer of every page
- [x] Is **clearly linked from every opt-in point** (e.g., next to every web form)

---

## Quick Reference — Footer Checklist

A simple way to catch most of these requirements: make sure your site **footer** (visible on
every page) includes:

- [x] Link to Privacy Policy
- [x] Link to Terms of Service
- [x] Visible contact info (or a link to a contact page)

---

## Notes

- This checklist covers **website requirements only**. RingCentral's full SMS registration also
  requires a separate **Brand registration checklist** (business/EIN verification) and a
  **Use case checklist** (describing how SMS will be used, with sample messages) — these are
  not website items, but will also be needed to complete registration.
- This document is for internal planning purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
  Consult legal counsel to confirm your Privacy Policy and Terms of Service language is
  appropriate for your business.
